## About

Backend engineer focused on turning complex workflows into maintainable systems.

My work is centered around backend architecture, automation, real-time communication, worker orchestration, media-processing workflows and the integration of AI agents with traditional software.

I care about **simple architecture, observability, resource efficiency and systems that actually operate in production**.

## What I work on

- **Backend architecture** — APIs, services, integrations and application infrastructure
- **Automation** — replacing repetitive operational work with reliable pipelines
- **Agentic systems** — coordinating AI agents, workers and conventional backend services
- **Distributed workflows** — queues, workers, scheduling and asynchronous processing
- **Real-time systems** — sockets, WebSockets and event-driven communication
- **Performance & efficiency** — building for constrained environments instead of assuming unlimited resources

## Current focus

I'm currently exploring how autonomous agents and deterministic software can work together: letting AI handle reasoning and coordination while conventional services provide predictable execution, validation and control.

I also continue to deepen my work with **Go**, especially concurrency, performance and backend architecture.
